Blackjack 02. Final scores

📅 2026-07-01🕐 6 mins🌎 ca🌎 en

A key aspect that makes Blackjack decision-making difficult is the fact that we always play our hand before the dealer does. It almost feels like bidding in a secret auction, where, without knowing how much our rival will bid, we need to place a bid high enough to win. Except that our rival does know how much we are bidding. This is called the last mover advantage.
